I'm a post-doc researcher in the [Prediction and Learning Lab](https://www.lawsonlab.co.uk/people) broadly interested in computational psychiatry. More specifically, I'm trying to leverage computational modelling of associative learning tasks to develop robust assays of uncertainty estimates.

 *specifically in using computational modelling of belief updating/associative learning to understand more about the brain basis of seemingly suboptimal behaviour.*

You can read more about me here, and what I have done here.

I'm trying to get into blogging/making notes on random topics, but I wrote a fairly extensive blog about PhD applications here.
